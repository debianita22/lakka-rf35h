**What's new**

- **Every core at its newest upstream.** The 22 RetroArch cores that v1.4.0
  had to keep at the versions Lakka pinned in May now compile at the newest
  commit of their upstream as well, among them Mupen64Plus-Next, ParaLLEl
  N64, Flycast, FBNeo, Beetle PSX, Citra, DeSmuME and PUAE; MAME, PPSSPP and
  SwanStation moved on too. The full list is below.
- **DOSBox is a different core now.** Its upstream rebuilt it on dosbox-core
  with additions from DOSBox Pure: a ZIP file opens as content without being
  extracted (the game's changes go to `<name>.save.zip` in the saves folder),
  CD and floppy images inside the ZIP are mounted, a start menu lists the
  programs when there is more than one, games it knows get their keys mapped
  to the gamepad, and Roland SC-55 MIDI works with its ROMs in the system
  folder. Its core options are new: settings made for the old DOSBox don't
  carry over. DOSBox-core, DOSBox Pure and DOSBox SVN are unchanged.
- **SAME CDi** adds disc control and M3U playlists for multi-disc games, a
  PAL/NTSC option and the Digital Video Cartridge (MPEG video).
- **EasyRPG Player** comes with the newer liblcf it needs, the library that
  reads RPG Maker games; the two are now updated together.

Coming from v1.3.0 or earlier: v1.4.0 updated 131 cores and compiled the
kernel for the console's CPU, see the
[v1.4.0 notes](https://github.com/debianita22/lakka-rf35h/releases/tag/v1.4.0);
v1.3.0 fixed standby and brought 162 RetroArch cores instead of 34, see the
[v1.3.0 notes](https://github.com/debianita22/lakka-rf35h/releases/tag/v1.3.0).
